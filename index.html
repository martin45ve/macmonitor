What Sensors adds

The App Store version of MacMonitor can't read these on its own. The helper can.

Chip temperatureThe hottest core, live.
Fan speedAverage of all fans, in RPM.
PowerWhole-Mac watts, plus GPU and Neural Engine.
Every coreSpeed of each CPU core and the GPU.

Privacy

MacMonitor and MacMonitor Sensors collect no data. Nothing is sent to the internet, there are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

Sensor readings stay on your Mac: the helper shares them only with MacMonitor, through a private folder that both apps use.

MacMonitor asks for Location access only because macOS shows Wi-Fi network names just to apps that have it. Your location is never read, stored or shared.

Support

To remove the helper: open MacMonitor Sensors from Applications, click Quit Sensors, then move it to the Trash. You can also turn it off in System Settings → General → Login Items.

Questions or problems? Open a request on GitHub Issues. Right-click a panel → Export Diagnostics and attach the file — it helps a lot.
